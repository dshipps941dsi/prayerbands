# Prayer Bands help guide

This is what the help assistant knows. Keep it plain and accurate. Every
feature and how to do it, with the page it lives on. Links in square
brackets are the only pages the assistant may send people to.

## What Prayer Bands is

A Prayer Band is a silicone wristband with a tiny NFC chip and a unique ID
(like PB-ABC12), shown at the top of its page and in the order email. Tap it to the back of a phone and its page
opens: you see the prayers people have attached to it and add your own. A band
is meant to be worn, prayed over, and passed on, so it builds a chain of prayer
from person to person. There is no battery, no charging, nothing to install,
and the band does not track location. Waterproof.

- Learn more: [How it works](/how-it-works)
- Common questions: [FAQ](/faq)

## Getting a band

- Buy bands in the store. Choose a color or a themed design (mountain, beach,
  military, breast cancer, baseball, golf, volleyball, and more) and a size.
  Free shipping on orders of $35 or more. Orders usually ship within 3 business
  days and arrive 5 to 7 business days after that. [Store](/store)
- Give one as a gift: at checkout you can add a name and a personal note for
  each band. The note waits for the person until they tap the band. You can
  change the note later from My Bands. [Store](/store)
- Bulk orders for a church, youth group, team, or ministry: the store has a
  Bulk Order tab where you pick styles and sizes in one grid. Larger quantities
  are discounted. Bulk orders ship to you to hand out. [Store](/store)
- Subscriptions ship new bands on a schedule (monthly, quarterly, or a monthly
  bundle) at a discount. Change color and size any time, cancel any time, no
  fee. Bands from a subscription are credited to you to give away.
  [Subscribe](/subscribe)
- Ship each band to a different person: normally an order ships to one address.
  We can mail each band to a different address for $4.99 per extra address.
  Place the order, then send the names and addresses through the contact form.
  [Contact](/contact)
- Referrals: every account has a referral code and link. When someone orders
  with it, you earn store credit that comes off your next order automatically.
  Your code and balance are under Account in the app. [Account](/my-band?tab=account)

## Registering a band and leaving a prayer

- Tap the band with your phone (iPhone: hold the band to the top back of the
  phone; Android: the middle of the back). A notification opens the band's
  page. If tapping does not work, type the ID from your order email at
  [Register](/register).
- Say who is wearing it: you, or someone else you are registering it for.
  Write a prayer if you like. That adds a "stop" to the band's journey.
- No account is needed to tap a band and leave a prayer.
- You can edit or add your prayer later from your stop on the band's journey.

## Accounts and signing in

- A free account keeps everything together: your bands, your prayer journal,
  your partners, your circles, and your inbox, on any phone.
- Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Google. Apple
  sign-in is on the sign-in page too. There is no password to remember.
  [Sign in](/signin)
- Under 13: a parent or guardian creates the account.
- Your band is not showing on your account: tap the band while signed in. If
  it was registered in your name, the page asks "Is it yours?" Tap Yes and it
  attaches. If you registered it and created your account within half an hour,
  it attaches by itself.
- Settings: avatar, name, sign-in method, notifications. Open Account in the
  app, then Settings. [Account](/my-band?tab=account)
- Install the app: open prayerbands.com in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome
  (Android), tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On iPhone that is also what
  turns on push notifications.

## The app (My Band)

Signed in, [My Band](/my-band) opens your band. The bottom bar has Home,
Journey, Purchase, Account, and Help.

- Home: the daily verse, your band's prayers, and the prayer panel with three
  tabs: My Journal, My Partners, My Circles.
- Journey: the map and chain of everyone who has worn or prayed over your band,
  and your whole reach across all your bands.
- Purchase: the store.
- Account: inbox, My Bands, settings, store credit and referral code, sign out,
  and the link to the full website.
- Several bands: the switcher at the top of the band page moves between the
  bands you are wearing and the ones you have to give away.

## My Journal

Your own prayer journal. Private unless you choose to share a prayer.
[Open My Journal](/my-band?open=requests)

- "+ Prayer" writes an entry. Give it a title ("Mom's surgery") and the
  details underneath. Choose Prayer, Note, or Verse. A verse has a reference
  and a "Today's verse" button that fills in the daily verse.
- Every entry is dated and time-stamped and the journal reads by day.
- "+ Update" under an entry adds a dated follow-up ("surgery went well").
- "Mark answered" asks how it was answered and keeps that with the prayer.
  Reopen from the entry's menu if needed.
- Edit or remove an entry from its "..." menu.
- Lists: "+ List" shows your lists and "Create list" (Family, Health, Work).
  The Filter picker shows one list at a time.
- Sharing a prayer: when writing a prayer choose "Just me", "All my partners",
  or one of your partner groups. Shared prayers show how many people have
  prayed. Notes and verses are always private.

## Prayer Partners

Partners are the people you are connected to in prayer: a friend, a spouse, a
small group. [Open My Partners](/my-band?open=partners)

- Connect three ways, from My Partners, "Connect a prayer partner":
  1. Tap phones: hold your bands to each other's phones.
  2. Scan: show your QR code and let them scan it.
  3. Code: read them the code from the top of your band's page, or type in theirs.
- The other person accepts the request from their inbox.
- Direct partners are people you connected with directly. Lineage partners are
  people a band actually passed between.
- Groups: sort partners into private groups (Youth Group, Baseball Team) that
  only you see. Then share a prayer with just that group.
- Their Requests: prayers your partners and circles have shared. Tap the
  praying hands to say "I prayed". Mute anyone whose requests you would rather
  not see; they are not told.
- Send a partner a quick "prayed for you" note from their row.
- Partners see only what you share. Your journal stays private.

## Prayer Circles

A circle is a group that prays together: a family, a small group, a team, a
ministry. Everyone reads the same prayer wall. [Open My Circles](/my-band?open=circles)

- Create a circle: My Circles, "Create a circle", give it a name and a
  description. You become its leader. You need a band to create one.
- Invite people: the circle's card shows its join code. Share the code, the
  link, or the QR code.
- Join a circle: enter the join code under My Circles, or open the link you
  were sent. You need a free account to see a circle.
- The prayer wall is forum style: topics (requests and updates) with prayers
  underneath. The leader and co-leaders post topics. Members who have a band
  write prayers under a topic. Anyone following without a band can tap to say
  they prayed.
- Co-leaders: the leader can name any member a co-leader from the Members
  section. Co-leaders can post topics, edit the circle's name and description,
  and remove members.
- Answered: a topic can be marked answered and reopened.
- Leaving: Settings inside the circle. The leader can make a new join code or
  close the circle.
- Notifications: new topics, prayers under your topic, and (for leaders) new
  members arrive in your inbox and as push notifications if you turned them on.

Partners versus circles: partners are one-to-one connections you make
yourself; a circle is a group with a leader and one shared wall.

## Giving a band away (passing it on)

- Open the band, tap "Pass on", and write a note for the person. Then hand
  them the band.
- The hand-off is pending until they tap the band with their own phone. That
  tap makes it theirs and shows them your note.
- You stay in the band's story as the one who gave it, and you become Lineage
  partners.
- If a week passes with no tap, you get a reminder. Ask them to tap it, or
  cancel the hand-off from the band page if plans changed.
- Bands you bought to hand out sit under My Bands as "to give away" until each
  person taps theirs. A band addressed to someone at checkout reads
  "for Sarah". You do not need to register those bands yourself.
  [My Bands](/my-band?tab=account)

## Inbox and notifications

- The inbox (Account, Inbox) holds partner requests, prayers shared with you,
  "prayed for you" notes, circle activity, and hand-off reminders.
- Push notifications: turn them on in Settings. On iPhone the app must be
  added to the Home Screen first.

## Prayer Wall, map, and verse

- Public Prayer Wall: prayers people chose to share publicly, anonymously or

  [Prayer Wall](/prayer-wall)
- Daily verse: a new verse every day on the home page and in the app.
  [Today's verse](/verse)

## Privacy

- The band cannot track anyone. The pins on the map come from the city a person
  types in when registering, or a rough city-level guess from their connection.
- We never sell personal information. [Privacy policy](/privacy)
- Request a copy of your data, or deletion, at hello@prayerbands.com.
