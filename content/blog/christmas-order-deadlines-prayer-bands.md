Every December we get the same message on the 20th: "Can these still make it?" Sometimes yes. Here are the honest dates so you do not have to ask.

## The deadlines

| What you are ordering | Order by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Standard bands, any color or theme | **December 12** | They ship within three business days and arrive in five to seven. That leaves a week of margin for the carrier. |
| Custom bands with your verse engraved | **Already past** | Custom bands take four to six weeks. Orders placed now are not guaranteed by Christmas. See below. |
| Bulk orders for a church or team, standard designs | **December 5** | Larger orders take longer to pack and ship as several boxes. |
| Gift notes added at checkout | **Any time** | The note lives on the band's page, not in the box, so it can be written or changed after the band ships. |

Shipping is free on orders of $35 or more, and every order gets a tracking number the day it leaves.

## If you missed the custom deadline

You have two good options.

**Order a standard band now and write the verse in the note.** Pick the color or theme that fits them. At checkout, add their name and put the verse you wanted engraved in the personal note. When they tap the band, the verse and your words are the first thing they see. Most people prefer this to an engraving anyway, because the note is longer than anything that fits on a band.

**Give the band at Christmas and have the custom one follow.** Order the standard band for under the tree and the custom band for January. The standard one is the one they tap and wear; the custom one arrives as a second gift when the engraving is done.

## If you are giving ten or more

Read [How to Give a Prayer Band at Christmas So It Actually Gets Worn](/blog/how-to-give-a-prayer-band-at-christmas) before the box arrives. The short version: write the note at checkout, hand the bands out last, and help everyone tap theirs in the room. The bands that go in a drawer are the ones that never got tapped.
